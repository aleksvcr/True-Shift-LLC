About TRUE SHIFT

The strategic foundation behind stronger service businesses.

TRUE SHIFT LLC serves as the strategic foundation for the development and management of multiple business initiatives.

Our purpose is to identify opportunities, create practical business structures, protect long-term value, and help companies operate more efficiently.

We focus primarily on industries connected to property services, environmental consulting, disaster recovery, restoration, business operations, and technology-enabled service delivery.

Our principle / 01

Create valuable businesses, build reliable systems, and maintain the flexibility to adapt when circumstances change.
